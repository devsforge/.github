Welcome to DevsForge! This document outlines how to get help, ask questions,
and connect with our community.

We communicate in both English and Ukrainian.

## Getting Help

Our community support operates on a best-effort basis. We are volunteers and
maintainers who contribute our time to help others. While we strive to respond
promptly, please be patient.

### GitHub Discussions (Primary)

For general questions, ideas, and community conversations, please use
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/devsforge/discussions).

This is our primary support channel and the best place to:

- Ask questions about DevsForge projects
- Share ideas and suggestions
- Connect with other community members
- Seek guidance on developer growth and learning

Not all repositories have discussions enabled individually, but organization-
level discussions are always available.

### GitHub Issues

For bug reports and feature requests, please use GitHub Issues in the relevant
repository.

When opening an issue, please:

- Search existing issues first to avoid duplicates
- Use a clear, descriptive title
- Provide as much relevant detail as possible
- Follow any issue templates provided in the repository

### Security Vulnerabilities

For reporting security vulnerabilities, please do not use public channels.
Instead, follow the process outlined in our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## Support Scope

### Technical Support

For technical questions related to DevsForge projects, tools, and
implementations, our support team is here to help.

Responsible team: @support

### Community & Mentorship

For general developer mentorship, career guidance, and community-related
questions, our community team can help.

Responsible team: @community

### Out of Scope

While we want to be as helpful as possible, some topics fall outside our
support scope:

- Support for third-party tools or services not maintained by DevsForge
- General programming questions unrelated to DevsForge projects
- Requests that violate our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
